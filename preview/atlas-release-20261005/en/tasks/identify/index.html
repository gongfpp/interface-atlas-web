Pick the parts in this order

Each stop explains the problem it solves and the trade-off to consider.

01

Sidebar ↗

A persistent column of navigation switches primary sections. A temporary panel may be a drawer; the interaction matters.

02

Card ↗

Group one object’s title, description and actions. Cards work for independent content; too many borders fragment continuous information.

03

Primary button ↗

A stronger fill emphasizes the next step. A task area usually needs one primary action; meaning must not depend on color alone.

04

Glassmorphism ↗

Background content shows through a blurred surface. This is a visual style, not a component; check text contrast over complex backgrounds.

Organic

有机自然

Aliases:自然系设计植物感网页大地色风格organic design

OverviewStyle labVariantsImplementationPrompts

What is it

A visual language of natural shapes, warm materials and muted earth tones. Soft contours and generous spacing create an unhurried rhythm while text and controls keep clear boundaries. It suits lifestyle and sustainability themes, provided organic decoration supports content without weakening readability or interaction.

Design spec

Typography

Serif headlines with clear sans-serif body text.

Color

Cream, moss and clay; deep green for body text.

Border

Soft radii and occasional arches; keep controls predictable.

Shadow

Gentle contact shadows without strong glow.

Spacing

Generous whitespace and leading, stacked on narrow screens.

Motion

250–350ms ease-out organic growth and fades; honour prefers-reduced-motion

States

Hover expands slightly and deepens the moss green; focus shows a visible green frame; active contracts one pixel; disabled fades to grey-green; error uses clay red

✓ When to use

  • Home, lifestyle, gardening and craft brands.
  • Content that calls for a warm, calm and comfortable atmosphere.

✕ When not to use

  • Avoid elaborate organic decoration in dense transactional interfaces.
  • Avoid pale text over textured surfaces.
